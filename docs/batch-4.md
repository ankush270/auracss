## 🏢 16. Corporate Theme (`data-theme="corporate"`)
Professional, brand-aligned enterprise design system with crisp grid lines, Poppins headers, and Open Sans body text.

---

## 🌌 17. Cosmic Theme (`data-theme="cosmic"`)
Futuristic sci-fi dark space aesthetic (`#0B0E17`) with Audiowide font and neon blue/purple radial glow halos.

### Example Usage:
```html
<div data-theme="cosmic">
  <div class="theme-card">
    <span class="theme-badge">Spatial Interface</span>
    <h2>Futuristic System</h2>
    <button class="theme-btn">Launch Core</button>
  </div>
</div>
```

---

## 🎨 18. Creative Theme (`data-theme="creative"`)
Character-driven comic book aesthetic with Bangers display font, thick `3px` black outlines, and hard blue offset box shadows.

---

## 👑 19. Dark Luxury Theme (`data-theme="dark-luxury"` or `data-theme="dark luxury"`)
Premium near-black warm background (`#0a0907`), amber gold accents (`#d4a03c`), inset top-highlight card borders, and Inter typography.

### Example Usage:
```html
<div data-theme="dark-luxury">
  <div class="theme-card">
    <span class="theme-badge">[AI FOUNDATION]</span>
    <h2>High-End AI Studio</h2>
    <button class="theme-btn">Get Access</button>
  </div>
</div>
```

---

## 🕹️ 20. Dithered Theme (`data-theme="dithered"`)
Nostalgic Game Boy-inspired dithering dot-pattern visual style with dashed borders and Space Grotesk typography.
