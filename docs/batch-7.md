## 📐 31. Geometric Theme (`data-theme="geometric"`)
Structured mathematical layout with clean lines, Inter typography, precise alignment, and neutral background tones.

---

## 💎 32. Glassmorphism Theme (`data-theme="glassmorphism"`)
Frosted glass aesthetic with translucent layers (`rgba(255,255,255,0.08)`), blur overlays (`backdrop-filter: blur(16px)`), and luminous borders.

### Example Usage:
```html
<div data-theme="glassmorphism">
  <div class="theme-card">
    <span class="theme-badge">Glass Layer</span>
    <h2>Translucent Panel</h2>
    <button class="theme-btn">Action Button</button>
  </div>
</div>
```

---

## 🌈 33. Gradient Theme (`data-theme="gradient"`)
Smooth purple (`#990FFA`) and electric pink (`#E60076`) gradient transitions with Space Grotesk display typography.

---

## 🌲 34. Immersive Theme (`data-theme="immersive"`)
Exhibit storytelling layout on a continuous Deep Emerald Green canvas (`#002914`) with Cobalt Blue secondary accents.

---

## 🖼️ 35. Impeccable Theme (`data-theme="impeccable"`)
Graphic editorial-poster aesthetic featuring sharp Amber (`#CC8800`) and burnt orange (`#C55221`) accents with Chakra Petch typography.
