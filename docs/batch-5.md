## ✏️ 21. Doodle Theme (`data-theme="doodle"`)
Playful hand-drawn sketch aesthetic with Delius Swash Caps handwritten font, imperfect borders (`2px solid #263D5B`), and hard offset box shadows.

---

## 🎭 22. Dramatic Theme (`data-theme="dramatic"`)
High-contrast theatrical dark mode (`#09090B`) featuring Outfit font with extreme weight scale (`400` vs `900`) and glowing purple/rose halos.

---

## 📰 23. Editorial Theme (`data-theme="editorial"`)
Refined magazine publication layout with Gelasio serif typography, crisp line dividers, and elegant reading rhythm.

---

## ☁️ 24. Enterprise Theme (`data-theme="enterprise"`)
Dark-themed cloud infrastructure dashboard aesthetic (`#09090B`), IBM Plex Sans font, glass-like panels (`backdrop-filter: blur(16px)`), and cloud blue accents (`#0C5CAB`).

---

## 💖 25. Expressive Theme (`data-theme="expressive"`)
Dynamic, personality-driven theme featuring deep pink (`#DB2777`) and vibrant blue (`#2563EB`) accents with IBM Plex Mono font throughout.
