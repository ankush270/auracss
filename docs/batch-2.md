## ⚡ 6. Bold Theme (`data-theme="bold"`)
High-impact dark background aesthetic (`#111111`) with Archivo Black heavyweight display typography and glowing blue accents.

### Example Usage:
```html
<div data-theme="bold">
  <div class="theme-card">
    <span class="theme-badge">Command Center</span>
    <h2>Heavyweight Title</h2>
    <button class="theme-btn">Execute</button>
  </div>
</div>
```

---

## 🏛️ 7. Brutalism Theme (`data-theme="brutalism"`)
Raw concrete architectural design with Darker Grotesque font, unadorned surfaces, terracotta red accents, and hard offset shadows.

---

## ⚡ 8. Brutalist (Neobrutalism) Theme (`data-theme="brutalist"`)
Modern Gumroad/Figma style neobrutalism. Features hard unblurred black shadows (`5px 5px 0px #000000`), thick `3px` black borders, and saturated pink, yellow, and blue primary accents.

### Example Usage:
```html
<div data-theme="brutalist">
  <div class="theme-card">
    <span class="theme-badge">Indie Creator</span>
    <h2>Buy Product - $29</h2>
    <button class="theme-btn">Checkout</button>
  </div>
</div>
```

---

## ☕ 9. Cafe Theme (`data-theme="cafe"`)
Warm, cozy espresso brown (`#5D4432`) and soft cream background (`#F9F7F5`) with smooth Poppins typography.

---

## 🖋️ 10. Claude Theme (`data-theme="claude"`)
Refined Anthropic-inspired research journal aesthetic on warm stone backdrop (`#FAF9F6`) with crisp near-black typography (`#141413`).
